How to book once we’ve picked

  1. Book the two flights separatelyThe way out and the way back are on different airlines, so they’re two one-way tickets on two websites.
  2. Book in small groups of 2–4Airlines price every seat in a booking at the same level. Searching for 11 at once can push everyone onto a more expensive fare.
  3. Add extras while bookingThe trolley and seat are cheapest during booking, not at the airport.
  4. Don’t wait too longThe days around Whitsun fill up first, and these prices were noticeably low for the season when checked.

Prices checked on 14 November 2026 on Google Flights and on the Pegasus, Transavia and Corendon websites, for 1 adult. Pegasus prices include the €15 check-in fee and €8 service fee its checkout adds, which its calendar view leaves out. They change daily, so treat them as a guide and check before paying.

Left out on purpose: TUI (€220–360 one way), Transavia and Corendon morning flights out (06:00–07:20), Pegasus flights back (they leave at 05:45), and anything landing at 22:00 or later.
